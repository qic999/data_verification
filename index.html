FILTERING POLICY

Data filtering rules

数据过滤规则

Known format, coordinate-axis, and scale conventions are corrected first. Only residual cases that meet a dataset-specific hard rule are filtered; borderline cases remain in the data and are sent to human review. 首先修正已知的数据格式、坐标轴和尺度约定。只有修正后仍满足数据集专用 hard rule 的样本才会被过滤；边界样本继续保留，并进入人工复核。

01

Normalize and repair

标准化并修正

Load with the current training loader, convert boxes to the camera frame, apply known axis/handedness fixes, and verify finite positive geometry.

使用当前训练 loader，将框转换到相机坐标系，应用已知的坐标轴和手性修正，并检查几何量有限且为正。
02

Measure projection consistency

计算投影一致性

Project the 3D cuboid with the loader intrinsics, clip at the image boundary, then compare it with the reference 2D box or visible mask.

使用 loader 内参投影 3D 框，在图像边界裁剪后，与参考 2D 框或可见 mask 比较。
03

Hard failure → Filtered

严重错误 → 过滤

Invalid geometry or a metric beyond a hard threshold is excluded from training and recorded in the deletion/filter manifest.

无效几何或超过 hard threshold 的样本不再参与训练，并记录到删除或过滤清单。
04

Borderline → Human review

边界样本 → 人工复核

A review threshold is a screening signal only. The sample is not deleted unless visual inspection confirms an annotation error.

review threshold 只用于筛查。除非可视化人工确认标注错误，否则样本不会被删除。
